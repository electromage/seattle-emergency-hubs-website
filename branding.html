The site uses the system font stack (Segoe UI, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif) — no web font download required. Match this stack in print or document materials where possible, or substitute a clean geometric sans-serif such as Open Sans or Source Sans Pro.

Usage Guidelines

Do
  • Use the provided logo files without modification
  • Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the icon mark
  • Use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds
  • Use Navy Blue or Forest Green as dominant brand colors
  • Credit Seattle Emergency Hubs when reproducing materials
  • Contact us before using the logo in partnership materials or press
Don't
  • Stretch, skew, or otherwise distort the logo
  • Change logo colors or apply gradients or drop shadows
  • Place the logo on a busy photographic background without a clear field
  • Use the logo to imply official endorsement without permission
  • Recreate the logo in a different typeface or icon
  • Use Warm Orange as a background color for large areas of text
