Washington state officially celebrates **Amateur Radio Month** throughout June. Governor Bob Ferguson proclaimed the month to recognize the state's nearly 33,000 amateur radio operators and their vital role in providing emergency communications during disasters.

Gather with Hams at Seattle's “Field Day” – June 26–28.

More info [HERE](https://www.seattleradiofieldday.org/index.html). [Register directly here.](https://events.humanitix.com/seattle-radio-field-day-2026)

For our non-ham friends, our [Get On The Air (GOTA) station](http://www.arrl.org/files/file/Field-Day/2026/2026%20FD%20GOTA%20Scoring%20FAQ.pdf) will have you on the air and experiencing HF radio. A licensed operator will sit with you and guide you through your first exchanges. Welcome to the world of radio!

For a complete directory of hamfests, club meetings, and local operating stations throughout the Pacific Northwest, you can search the [ARRL Hamfests and Conventions Calendar](http://www.arrl.org/hamfests-and-conventions-calendar).
