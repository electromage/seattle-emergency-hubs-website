Recent events, including the earthquake in Venezuela, are a reminder that emergencies can happen with little warning.

When phone, internet, or power systems are disrupted, it helps to already know where your neighborhood can regroup. Emergency Hubs are natural gathering spaces where neighbors can find information and other types of support when normal communication systems are down.

## Take One Minute Today

Find your nearest hub now and share it with your household so everyone knows where to go after a disaster:

[Find your nearest Hub](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1mtS4LK_-BMm2TqP2su-Y1cIZUOk&usp=sharing)

Then tell your neighbors too. A simple shared plan can make a real difference in the first hours after an emergency.

_Neighbors supporting neighbors is what makes communities resilient._
