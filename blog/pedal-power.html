Pedal Power!

June 2, 2026 · Seattle Emergency Hubs Team

What happens when roads are blocked, fuel is scarce, and neighbors still need help?

Sometimes the answer has two wheels.

Join Disaster Relief Trials Seattle 2026 for a fun, hands-on event showing how bicycles can support communities after a disaster.

Event Details

Saturday, July 25
9 a.m.
Register at: www.DisasterReliefTrials.com

What to Expect

  • Navigate the city to visit checkpoint stations where you will complete disaster-related tasks
  • Traverse up to 25-30 miles
  • Challenge yourself with bicycle-related disaster relief skills
  • Connect with others working to make our communities disaster resilient
  • Support research investigating bicycle use in disaster response (learn more at DisasterReliefTrials.com)

All bicycle types are welcome, and different levels of ride challenge and intensity are available - bring your family and cruise the checkpoints for fun and education, or test your capabilities by completing the full circuit.

Disaster Relief Trials Seattle 2026 is organized by the Disaster Relief Trials nonprofit and researchers from the University of Washington, Oklahoma State University, and the University of Virginia, with support from local community partners.
